# Points and rules

## Points per link, by the GIVER's verified authority

| Authority | Points | Band |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | 1 | Seed |
| 6–15 | 2 | Starter |
| 16–25 | 4 | Emerging |
| 26–35 | 6 | Growing |
| 36–45 | 9 | Established |
| 46–55 | 13 | Solid |
| 56–65 | 18 | Strong |
| 66–75 | 25 | Authority |
| 76–85 | 40 | High Authority |
| 86–100 | 60 | Elite |

The curve is steep on purpose. Authority scores are logarithmic — a DR 88 site is
not three times a DR 30 site, it is roughly ten times. The points reflect that.

## Relevance multiplier

| Match | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Same category | ×2 |
| Adjacent category (related, shared audience) | ×1.5 |
| Unrelated | ×1 |

Final value = band points × multiplier. A DR 60 site giving a same-niche link
earns 36 and costs the receiver 36.

## Why low-authority members should still play

A DR 30 site earns 12 per same-niche link. Three rounds gets you 36 — enough to
receive from a DR 56–65 site. Five rounds reaches the Authority band. Giving
consistently is how small sites climb; there is no other route.

## Membership states

| State | Can give | Can receive |
|---|---|---|
| Probation | Yes | No |
| Active | Yes | Yes |
| Paused | No | No |
| Removed | No | No |

Every new member starts on Probation. Two links delivered and still live after 30
days moves you to Active.

## Link rules

**One exchange link per post.** Never two. This is the single rule that keeps the
network invisible to spam classifiers — a post with one contextual outbound link
looks like writing, a post with four looks like a scheme.

**One link to any given domain per 180 days.**

**No direct swaps.** A links to B, B links to C, C links to A. 90-day cooldown
before any pair can repeat in either direction.

**No monthly cap.** Give as often as you publish. Volume limits itself naturally.

**Editorial placement only.** Inside a body paragraph of a real post.

**Plain dofollow.** `rel="nofollow"`, `"sponsored"` and `"ugc"` do not count.

**Host post quality.** 600+ words minimum, genuinely relevant to the link, fewer
than 15 total outbound links on the page.

**Stays live 90 days.** Links are re-checked at 30, 60 and 90 days. A removed link
reverses the points and usually ends the membership.

## Anchor text

Vary it. Rotate between descriptive phrases, brand plus context, and partial
matches. Never use the same exact-match keyword twice, and never use a keyword
you are yourself trying to rank for as an outbound anchor.
