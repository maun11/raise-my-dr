## 1. Launch message (post to the community / broadcast)

*Editorial backlinks from other B2B startups, without the outreach.*

Publish a post, put one link to another member inside it, earn points. Spend
points to get links pointing back at you. Roughly two given for one received, at
every authority level.

You never pick your partner and you never swap directly. Everyone sits in a
rotating ring: you link to the next site along, someone further round links to
you.

Verification is automatic. We crawl your sitemap every 15 minutes, find the post,
check it against the rules, and the points appear. You submit nothing.

*The rules:* one exchange link per post, in a body paragraph, plain dofollow.
Host post 600+ words, under 15 outbound links. The link stays live 90 days. No
monthly cap, so give as often as you publish. We measure authority ourselves and
take nobody's word for it.

It runs as a Claude skill, so there is no dashboard to learn. I built it because
I needed the links for Indexly (useindexly.com).
