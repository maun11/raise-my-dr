## [WHAT THIS IS — one paragraph]

Raise My DR is a points-based link exchange for B2B startups, run as a Claude skill.
You publish a post with one link to another member inside it. We find the post
ourselves, check it against the rules, and add points to your balance. You spend those
points to receive links to your own site. Partners are assigned in a rotating ring —
you link to Anna, Anna links to Ben, Ben links to you — so nothing is ever a direct
swap, and there is no marketplace where the strongest sites get picked first. You never
submit a URL, fill in a spreadsheet, or chase anyone by email.

---

## [HOW IT WORKS — 3 numbered steps]

### 1. Get your assignment

Ask the skill who you're linking to. It names your partner, explains what they do, and
tells you exactly how many points the link is worth before you write a word.

### 2. Publish as normal

Write the post you were going to write anyway. Put one link to your partner inside a
body paragraph, where it makes sense. That's the only change to your process.

### 3. Points appear

We crawl your sitemap every 15 minutes. When the post shows up, we check the link
against every rule and credit your balance. You submit nothing. Then spend the points
to receive links to your own pages.

## [POINTS TABLE]

**Section heading:** What a link is worth

**Intro line:** Points are based on the authority of the site giving the link. We
measure that ourselves — whatever you claim on the form is ignored.

| Your authority | Band | Points per link |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0–5 | Seed | 1 |
| 6–15 | Starter | 2 |
| 16–25 | Emerging | 4 |
| 26–35 | Growing | 6 |
| 36–45 | Established | 9 |
| 46–55 | Solid | 13 |
| 56–65 | Strong | 18 |
| 66–75 | Authority | 25 |
| 76–85 | High Authority | 40 |
| 86–100 | Elite | 60 |

Then a relevance multiplier:

| Match | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Same category | ×2 |
| Adjacent category — related, shared audience | ×1.5 |
| Unrelated | ×1 |

**Worked example (pull-quote or aside):** A site at authority 34 linking inside its own
niche earns 6 × 2 = 12 points per post.

**Why the curve is steep (one line, sits under the table):** Authority scores are
logarithmic, so a link from an 88 really is worth around ten links from a 30 — paying
them the same would just make the strong sites leave.

**Ratio line:** Across every band it works out at roughly two links given for one
received. Nobody extracts from the network without feeding it first.

## [THE RULES]

**Section heading:** The rules

**Lead line:** One rule holds the whole thing up: one exchange link per post, never two.
A post with one contextual link reads like writing. A post with four reads like a
scheme. Everything else supports that.

- One exchange link per post. Never two.
- Inside a body paragraph of a real article — never a footer, sidebar, nav or resources page.
- Plain dofollow. `nofollow`, `sponsored` and `ugc` don't count.
- Host post is 600+ words with fewer than 15 outbound links on the page.
- The link stays live for 90 days. We re-check at 30, 60 and 90.
- One link to any given domain per 180 days.
- No direct swaps. Assignments rotate in a ring, with a 90-day cooldown before any pair can repeat.
- No monthly cap. Give as often as you publish.
- Authority is measured by us, never taken from what someone claims.
- Every new member starts on probation: give links and earn from day one, spend only after two of your links have stayed live for 30 days.

## [FAQ]

**Is this a link scheme? Will it get me penalised?**

It's link building, so it's worth answering properly rather than waving it away.
Google's spam policies target links exchanged at scale for the sole purpose of moving
rankings — sitewide footer links, reciprocal A-links-to-B-links-to-A pairs, paid
placements, pages built as link dumps. The design here is aimed at each of those. Links
sit in a body paragraph of a genuine post, chosen because the sentence supports them.
It's one link per post, never two, which is what keeps a page reading like an article.
Nobody swaps directly: assignments rotate in a ring, and a pair can't repeat within 90
days, so your outbound profile doesn't mirror your inbound one. And every domain is
vetted before it can be assigned — minimum authority, six months old, real posts of 600+
words, low spam signals, no gambling, adult, pharma or essay mills, and no two accounts
sharing an owner. What we can't tell you is that any link building carries zero risk;
nobody can. What we can tell you is that the footprint this leaves is a handful of
contextual links a year from vetted, relevant sites, in posts you'd have published
anyway. If you'd rather do no link building at all, that's a legitimate position and
this isn't for you.

**How much work is this, really?**

One link inside a post you were already writing. The skill names your partner and drafts
the paragraph if you want it to. After you publish, you do nothing — no URL to submit,
no sheet to update, no reminder emails. Most members spend under a minute on the
exchange itself.

**Who else is in it? Will I be linking to junk?**

Every domain clears the gate before it can be assigned to anyone. Authority of at least
5, six months old, at least ten indexed pages, posts averaging 600+ words, under 15
outbound links per page, low spam signals, and excluded categories are excluded. Sites
are re-scored over time, so anything that decays or gets de-indexed is worth less
automatically. If you're assigned a partner you'd rather not link to, say so and you get
a different one.

**What's the catch? Why is it free?**

There isn't a paid tier. It costs us a crawler and a roster, and it only works if there
are enough good sites in it — charging would shrink the pool and make the whole thing
worse. The real cost to you is the discipline: you have to keep publishing, and you have
to leave the links up for 90 days.

**What if my partner removes my link?**

We re-check every link at 30, 60 and 90 days. A removed link reverses the points and
usually ends that membership. The same applies to you, which is why the rule is stated
up front rather than buried.

**How do I talk to a human?**

the occasional argument about anchor text. Join here: `https://chat.whatsapp.com/Ie1fcHaniWf6TVxpELek5M`
